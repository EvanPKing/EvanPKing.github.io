### **CupVoice**
**Jun 2026 | Berkeley AI Hackathon**

CupVoice is an AI-powered accessibility prototype for live sports, built from 0 to 1 in 24 hours with Raelene Chow, Aaron W., and Yiyao L. It turns fast-moving match events into clearer text, voice, and visual cues, so people who cannot fully rely on visuals or audio can follow a game.

I worked on the web prototype and frontend demo — the website flow, user interface, and demo experience — letting users choose a sport, follow simplified match updates, and see accessibility-focused outputs. The demo remained an early prototype, but we turned an idea into something people could interact with, pairing the software with a teammate's hardware concept model.
